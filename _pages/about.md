I received my M.S. in Computer Technology from [Tianjin University](https://www.tju.edu.cn/) and my B.S. in Digital Media Technology from [Shandong University](https://www.sdu.edu.cn/).

My research interests include **graph neural networks**, **anomaly detection**, **large language models**, and **natural language processing**. I have published papers at top venues including CIKM, IJCAI, ISWC, and TOSEM.
